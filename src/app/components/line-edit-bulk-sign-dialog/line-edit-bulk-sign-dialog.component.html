WARNING!

By submitting your signature in the field below, you are approving all black and red lines. If there are already approvals on any line edits, your signature will overwrite the previous approval. If this is not your intention, please click "Cancel" to close this dialog.

Please note that you cannot approve red or black lines that you submitted. If you have not yet done so, it is advised that you review all the line edits before approving them via this dialog.

Note that red lines can only be approved within the red-lined run; if you are approving from a locked run, your approval will apply only to the black lines.

If you wish to proceed, please enter your APL 521 and individual pin in the field below. If you do not know your pin you may view it on your profile page by closing this dialog and opening the site menu in the top right corner of the page to navigate to your profile.
